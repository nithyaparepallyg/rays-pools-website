About us

Three decades of crystal clear water.

Rays Pools & Chemicals was founded in 1994 by Mr. A. S. Venkatesha. Since then, the Bengaluru firm has designed, built and refurbished more than 500 swimming pools and water bodies across India, for homeowners, hotels, builders, institutions and architects.

We plan every pool around how you'll actually use it — your site, your lifestyle and your budget. Our team of architects, engineers and technicians works alongside your architect from day one, and we stay on after handover with maintenance, equipment and chemical supply.

Ecology and conservation matter to us. Our design and research team works closely with architects and engineers to make every new pool more eco-friendly and sustainable.

How we work

Plan. Design. Build. Maintain.

Most pool projects go wrong in the gaps between the architect, the civil contractor, the plumber and the tile supplier. We remove the gaps by doing all of it ourselves — and keeping you informed at every stage.

  1. Concept & engineering

    Feasibility study, concept design, architectural drawings and structural engineering — all coordinated so every element works together.

  2. Construction

    Our own team manages the build end to end, so the project runs smoothly and on schedule.

  3. Plumbing & filtration

    Plant rooms, pumps, filters and pipework installed by skilled, background-verified plumbers.

  4. Tiles, lighting & finishes

    Full range of pool tiles in partnership with Sisa Mosaic, plus licensed underwater lighting.

  5. Handover & training

    We hand over a working pool and train your staff on daily care, dosing and equipment.

  6. Maintenance & AMC

    Annual maintenance plans for pools, fountains and water features — clean, balanced water all year.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us directly.

Do you build swimming pools outside Bengaluru?

Yes. We're based in Bengaluru and have built more than 500 pools and water bodies across India, including Mangaluru, Udupi, Manipal, Kundapura, Chikkamagaluru, Hampi, Hyderabad and Tamil Nadu.

What types of swimming pools do you build?

Residential and commercial pools of every kind: infinity pools, lap pools, terrace and rooftop pools, indoor pools, recreational pools, Olympic and mini-Olympic pools, spas and jacuzzis, above-ground pools and water parks — plus fountains and water features.

Can you work with our architect or builder?

Yes. Much of our work is with architects, engineers, builders and developers. Our design team joins early to coordinate the pool's structure, plumbing, filtration and finishes with the rest of the building.

Do you repair and maintain existing pools?

Yes. We repair, renovate and re-tile existing pools, and offer annual maintenance contracts (AMC) for pools, fountains and water features at homes, apartments, hotels and institutions.

Do you sell pool chemicals and equipment?

Yes. We supply chlorine and other organic and inorganic pool chemicals, test kits, and equipment including pumps, filters, cleaners, heaters, lights and valves.
